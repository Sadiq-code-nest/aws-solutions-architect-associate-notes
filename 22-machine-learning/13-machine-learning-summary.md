| Service | Category | Input | Output | What It Does | Typical Use Case | Key Exam Keywords |

## ⚠️ Most Common Exam Confusions

---

### 🔥 1. Polly vs Transcribe

- Polly → speaks
- Transcribe → listens

---

### 🔥 2. Lex vs Connect

- Lex → chatbot brain
- Connect → full call center

---

### 🔥 3. Comprehend vs Translate

- Comprehend → understand text
- Translate → change language

---

### 🔥 4. Kendra vs Personalize

- Kendra → search
- Personalize → recommend

---

### 🔥 5. SageMaker vs Everything

- SageMaker → build custom ML
- Others → pre-built AI services
