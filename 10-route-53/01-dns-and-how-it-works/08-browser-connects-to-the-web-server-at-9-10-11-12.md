        ### Key Terms Simply

        | Term | Simple Meaning |
        | --- | --- |
        | **Cache + TTL** | Remember the answer for X seconds, then forget |
        | **Root DNS** | Top of the hierarchy, knows who manages `.com`, `.org` etc. |
        | **TLD DNS** | Knows who manages specific domains under `.com` |
        | **SLD DNS** | The actual DNS for YOUR domain — has the real IP |
        | **Local DNS** | Your middleman — asks everyone on your behalf |

        ---

        ### One Line Summary

        > Your browser → Local DNS → Root → TLD → SLD → gets IP → connects ✅
