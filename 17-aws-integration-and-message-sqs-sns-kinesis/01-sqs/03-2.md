# Standard vs FIFO

| Feature | Standard | FIFO |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Throughput | Unlimited | Limited (300 msg/s, 3000 with batching) |
| Ordering | Best effort | Guaranteed |
| Duplicate Messages | Possible | Exactly once (deduplication) |
| Speed | Very High | Lower |
| Default Choice | ✅ Yes | Only when order matters |
| Example | Orders, image processing, email jobs | Bank transactions, stock trading, financial ledgers |

# Exam Scenarios

### Scenario 1

> Your application receives unpredictable traffic spikes, and you don't want requests to be lost.
> 

✅ **Amazon SQS Standard Queue**

---

### Scenario 2

> Bank transactions must be processed exactly in the order received, with no duplicates.
> 

✅ **Amazon SQS FIFO Queue**

---

### Scenario 3

> A consumer needs 2 minutes to process a message, but the visibility timeout is only 30 seconds.
> 

✅ Increase the **Visibility Timeout** or call **ChangeMessageVisibility**.

---

### Scenario 4

> Consumers frequently poll an empty queue, causing unnecessary API calls and cost.
> 

✅ Enable **Long Polling** (up to 20 seconds).

---

### Scenario 5

> Your application needs to send a 50 MB video through SQS.
> 

❌ **Don't put the video in SQS.**

✅ Upload the video to **Amazon S3**, then send the **S3 object URL** in the SQS message.

# 🎯 30-Second Memory Trick

Think of **Amazon SQS as the waiting line at a supermarket**:

- 🧑 **Producer** = Customer joins the line (sends a message).
- 🛒 **SQS Queue** = The waiting line that safely holds customers/messages.
- 👨‍💼 **Consumer** = Cashier processes one customer/message at a time.
- 👻 **Visibility Timeout** = While a cashier is serving a customer, no other cashier can take that same customer.
- ⏳ **Long Polling** = Instead of repeatedly asking "Any customers?", the cashier waits up to 20 seconds for one to arrive.
- 📦 **Standard Queue** = Fast, but messages may arrive out of order and occasionally be duplicated.
- 🔒 **FIFO Queue** = Slower, but guarantees order and exactly-once processing.

**One golden exam rule:**

> **Need maximum throughput? → Standard Queue.**
> 
> 
> **Need strict ordering and no duplicates? → FIFO Queue.**
>
