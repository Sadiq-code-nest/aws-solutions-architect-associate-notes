# Q

## A streaming company wants to share specific AWS resources with an external AWS account. However, they want to ensure that their data is secure and only the intended recipient can access the shared resources. As a solutions architect, how can you accomplish this?

Create an IAM user in your AWS account with full access and share the credentials with the external account.

Use Resource Policies to allow a specific AWS account access to the designated resources.

Share access keys of your resources with the external account, allowing them to directly access your resources.

Enable VPC peering between your account and the external account, allowing them to access all resources within your VP
